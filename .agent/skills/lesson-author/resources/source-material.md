# Buddhist Source Material Reference

A curated library of source material for lesson development, organized by category.

## Jataka Tales (Buddha's Previous Lives)

These are ancient stories from the Pali Canon describing the Buddha's past lives, often as animals. Best suited for ages 0-7 (as simple tales) and 8-12 (with moral analysis).

| # | Tale | Core Teaching | Recommended Tier |
|---|------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | The Monkey King (Mahakapi Jataka) | Self-sacrifice, leadership, protecting others | 0-7, 8-12 |
| 2 | The Rabbit on the Moon (Sasa Jataka) | Dana (generosity), giving from the heart | 0-7 |
| 3 | The Deer King (Nigrodhamiga Jataka) | Compassion, protecting the vulnerable | 0-7, 8-12 |
| 4 | The Golden Swan (Suvanna Hamsa Jataka) | Greed destroys blessings, contentment | 0-7, 8-12 |
| 5 | The Elephant and the Blind Men | Perspective, humility, not judging | 8-12 |
| 6 | The Wise Quail (Sammodamana Jataka) | Unity, cooperation vs. division | 0-7 |
| 7 | The Banyan Deer (Nigrodha Jataka) | Self-sacrifice, mercy, ruling with compassion | 0-7, 8-12 |
| 8 | The Parrot and the Fig Tree | Loyalty, perseverance | 0-7 |
| 9 | The Tortoise and the Geese | Listening to advice, self-control of speech | 0-7, 8-12 |
| 10 | Prince Siddhartha and the Swan | Compassion for all life, resolving conflict peacefully | 0-7 |
| 11 | The Elephant Girly-Face | The influence of companions, choosing friends wisely | 8-12 |
| 12 | The Lion and the Boar | Futility of conflict, peace over pride | 8-12 |

## Buddhist Core Concepts

For 8-12 (simplified) and 13+ (with depth and analogy).

| Concept | Pali Term | 8-12 Analogy | 13+ Depth |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Four Noble Truths | Cattāri Ariyasaccāni | Video game treadmill, boredom cycle | Existential dissatisfaction, hedonic adaptation |
| Noble Eightfold Path | Ariya Aṭṭhaṅgika Magga | GPS for life, sports training plan | Integrated ethical-mental framework |
| Impermanence | Anicca | Sandcastles, seasons, trends | Identity flux, social media ephemerality |
| Non-self | Anatta | River that looks the same but water changes | Ship of Theseus, constructed identity |
| The Middle Way | Majjhima Paṭipadā | Not too tight/loose guitar string | Avoiding extremes in diet, study, relationships |
| Karma | Kamma | Seeds you plant → garden you grow | Intentional action, moral responsibility |
| Samsara | Saṃsāra | Merry-go-round, repeating patterns | Cyclic existence, habitual patterns |
| Three Marks of Existence | Tilakkhaṇa | Three "superpowers" of understanding | Philosophical framework for reality |
| Five Precepts | Pañca Sīla | Rules for being a good human | Ethical foundation, voluntary commitment |
| Loving-kindness | Mettā | Warm wishes for everyone, even people you don't like | Systematic compassion practice, breaking barriers |
| Dependent Origination | Paṭiccasamuppāda | Domino chain, everything connected | Causal web, emptiness of independent existence |
| Three Poisons | Akusala Mūla | Greed = grabbing, Hatred = pushing away, Delusion = fog | Root causes of suffering, psychological insight |
| Mindfulness | Sati | Camera that just watches without judging | Present-moment awareness, metacognition |
| Right Speech | Sammā Vācā | Think before you text/post | Digital ethics, social media responsibility |
| Equanimity | Upekkhā | Weatherman doesn't get mad at rain | Non-reactive awareness, emotional maturity |
